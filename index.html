The Lei Áurea in Akoma Ntoso

Brazil’s Law No. 3,353 of May 13, 1888, known as the Lei Áurea or Golden Law, which abolished slavery in Brazil. It is marked up in Akoma Ntoso, the OASIS standard for legal and legislative documents, and styled after the manuscript, with an English translation.

View the Lei Áurea

How it was made

The XML was generated by Claude for Grant Vergottini from the text published by the Câmara dos Deputados. The arms, title, initial and signatures come from the scan of the manuscript in the Arquivo Nacional, and the handwriting is approximated with script fonts. Claude didn’t write a word of the Portuguese text. It marked up the words of the source. The English translation is Claude’s own, is labeled as such, and isn’t an official text.
